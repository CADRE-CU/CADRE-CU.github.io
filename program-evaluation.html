Program Evaluation

Mixed-methods evaluations of state and district initiatives — from multi-tiered supports and social-emotional learning to instructional materials and school turnaround — built to answer the questions decision-makers actually face.

Overview

Good evaluation starts with the decision someone has to make, then works backward to the evidence that would change it.

CADRE conducts external evaluations and embedded research for state agencies, school districts, and foundations. Recent and current work includes the Colorado Department of Education's Multi-Tiered System of Supports, the state's K-5 social and emotional health pilot, high-quality instructional materials adoption in Adams 12, project-based learning in Pueblo 60, and the use of performance assessments to certify graduation readiness. Earlier work evaluated Denver's ProComp teacher compensation system and CU Boulder's online summer courses.

Our evaluations combine administrative and assessment data with surveys, interviews, focus groups, and observation, because implementation almost always explains outcomes. We work as partners over multiple years, report findings in language practitioners can use, and stay attentive to whether the evidence supports the causal claims people want to draw from it.

Questions guiding our work

  • Is the program being implemented as designed, and what conditions in schools help or hinder it?
  • What outcomes changed for students and educators, and how confident can we be that the program caused them?
  • Which practices distinguish schools that sustain improvement from those that do not?
  • What do district and state leaders need to know, and when, for the findings to shape their next decision?

Lead

Who's leading this work at CADRE.

ED

Elena Diaz-Bilello

Associate Director, CADRE · Assistant Research Professor, Research & Evaluation Methodology

Elena Diaz-Bilello works with state agencies, school districts, and educational organizations to design and evaluate reforms in assessment and educational policy. She draws on prior roles as a Senior Associate at the National Center for the Improvement of Educational Assessment, a program evaluator for Denver Public Schools, and a consultant to the Colorado Department of Education, experience that spans the district, state, and national levels.

She is principal investigator on CADRE's evaluations of Colorado's Multi-Tiered System of Supports, the K-5 social and emotional health pilot, high-quality instructional materials in Adams 12, Pueblo 60's Project Heroes, and the state's graduation-readiness performance assessment work. She holds a PhD in Research and Evaluation Methodology from CU Boulder.

Methods & approach

How we answer these questions.

We match the design to the decision: rigorous enough to be trusted, practical enough to be used, and reported on the timeline the partner actually works on.

Mixed-methods design Surveys, interviews, focus groups, and observation alongside administrative and assessment data
Quasi-experimental analysis Comparison groups, longitudinal cohorts, and descriptive outcome analyses where randomization is not possible
Implementation & case study Multi-site case studies that explain how and why programs work in particular schools
Utilization-focused reporting Interim briefings, practitioner-facing reports, and technical assistance alongside findings
