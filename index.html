Better evidence for the decisions that shape student learning.

CADRE is a research center in CU Boulder's School of Education. Our work spans classroom assessment, educational measurement, accountability, higher education, program evaluation, and technical advice to assessment programs, carried out with states, districts, and educational organizations across the country.

Explore our work Partner with us
Who we are

Based in the Research & Evaluation Methodology program, CADRE is a hub where expertise in assessment design, psychometrics, program evaluation, and education policy can be located and engaged.

We work with state departments of education, school districts, national assessment organizations, and curriculum publishers to produce research and tools that help educators understand what and how students are learning — and translate that understanding into better decisions.

Where our work lands
  1. ClassroomsFormative and performance assessment, and growth reports teachers can act on
  2. Schools & districtsProgram evaluation, instructional materials, and school turnaround
  3. StatesAccountability systems, growth models, statewide evaluations, and technical advisory committees
  4. National assessmentNAEP validity studies and score reporting with assessment developers
  5. Colleges & federal aidFAFSA, Title IV, and who gets to and through college
Our focus areas

Six strands of work that shape how learning is assessed and supported.

Each area connects a body of research to active partnerships with practitioners. Click through to see the projects, publications, and people working in that space.

Classroom Assessment

How teachers and students use formative and performance assessment to make learning visible and act on it.

Educational Measurement

The technical foundations of testing: psychometric modeling, scaling and growth, validity, and score interpretation.

Accountability

Growth models and school performance frameworks that fairly reflect what schools and educators contribute to learning.

Higher Education

Financial aid, FAFSA policy, and college enrollment and completion, with a focus on historically underserved students.

Program Evaluation

Mixed-methods evaluations of state and district initiatives, from MTSS and SEL to instructional materials, that inform real decisions.
